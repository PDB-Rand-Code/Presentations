Absorption of the April Tax Shift Savings

In April 2026, policy levies were shifted to general taxation (~2.5p–3.0p/kWh savings on paper), but were completely offset by three factors:

1. Wholesale Surge

Gas and electricity wholesale pricing pressures wiped out tax relief by October 2026.

2. Grid Upgrades

Ofgem RIIO-3 capital expenditure allowances raised high-voltage transmission charges.

3. WHD Restructuring

Warm Home Discount funding moved away from standing charges into per-kWh unit rates.
